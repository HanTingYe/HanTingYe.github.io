I earned the M.S. degree from the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China ([UESTC](https://en.uestc.edu.cn/)), and the B.S. degree from Chongqing University ([CQU](https://english.cqu.edu.cn/)).

### Research interests

I am passionate about leveraging ubiquitous mobile devices as multisensory interfaces to enable mobile AI systems that can perceive, understand, and interact with users and the physical world. I have identified and formalized several types of useful physical information in the optical domain by leveraging pervasive components such as displays and cameras across emerging mobile-device form factors. I am also interested in how different types of physical information interact to generate new insights for applying AI to specific tasks, while ensuring the efficient, responsive, robust, and trustworthy operation of deployed mobile systems in real-world environments. Moving forward, I aim to enable multimodal perception on emerging egocentric mobile platforms, e.g., smart glasses, and leverage physical insights to augment AI and robots and support humans in applications such as personal health management.
